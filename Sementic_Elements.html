    3. AI-powered surveillance system helps avert
    forest fire in Hosur division


    The AI cameras equipped with real time image processing and anomaly detection flagged the presence of flames and smoke prompting an emergency response within seconds



    An Artificial Intelligence-powered surveillance and a rapid response system helped avert a major forest fire in Jawalagiri forest range here in Hosur forest division, according to the Forest Department.


    Three fire incidents were detected by the Forest Department in real time with the aid of the AI driven monitoring system in Palayam and Geejanakuppam areas in Jawalagiri range. The AI cameras equipped with real time image processing and anomaly detection flagged the presence of flames and smoke prompting an emergency response within seconds, the Forest Department said. With the timely detection, the field staff of Jawalagiri range doused the fires. According to the Department, the teams were mobilised “within a record time of 35 minutes” and the fires were put out completely.
